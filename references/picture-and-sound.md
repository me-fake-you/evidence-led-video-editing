# Picture and sound decisions

## Correction before decoration

Inspect every selected photo; save full-resolution versioned derivatives with correction reasons, settings and before/after evidence. Distinguish natural haze, intended blur, low exposure, color cast, focus miss and motion blur. Preserve real highlights and depth. Avoid one preset for a batch and avoid masking corrected images with a full-frame dark overlay for text.

For video, inspect metadata and known camera settings before applying a technical color transform. A flat appearance alone does not establish a Log profile. Unknown color handling remains an explicit risk. Match adjacent shots by appearance and scopes where available, then recheck the encoded output. Do not infer color correctness merely from a BT.709 tag.

Stabilization should preserve intentional tracking or pans. Prefer a usable subrange or alternate shot over severe crop, blur or warped background. Report linear width/height retention, area retention and fill scale separately; for multiple transformations use actual cumulative geometry, and for dynamic transformations report range/curve. Mathematical crop accounting does not replace full-motion inspection of breathing, jelly, edge artifacts or subject loss.

## Photos and framing

Select stillness, push, pan, crop detail, composition match or collage for a specific visual purpose. Protect key regions; test at target display size. Full image on a designed field is preferable to a destructive crop when needed. One image with a detail crop is not two independent source photos. Reject poor 2.5D masks instead of inventing missing travel details.

## Narration and music

Plan voice, song and location-sound roles before filling the film with words. Preserve the user's vocal-song preference when present; schedule lyrics and narration to take turns, use a legitimately available instrumental section/stem when appropriate, or revise timing. Do not silently replace the chosen genre with generic instrumental music.

Record selected voice take, exact text version, provider/model where known, settings, audio hash, actual duration and pronunciation issues. Measure chosen audio before final timing. File-duration sum, placed interval union and estimated speech activity are different quantities; VAD/alignment is an estimate, not proof of perceived speech or intentional silence. Rewriting or extending a supported shot is usually preferable to forcing a long sentence into a short slot.

When editing one sentence, check neighboring timbre, pace, tails and loudness. Alignment to the correct selected take supports subtitle timing but needs review; manual timing is valid when alignment tools are unavailable. Test semantic line breaks, reading load and target UI overlap without assuming one universal characters-per-second threshold.

Distinguish synced recording, same-place nonsynchronous ambience and designed sound. Labels in metadata alone do not fix a misleading portrayal. Review private conversations and noise separately. J/L cuts are valid when they do not falsely establish a specific event or causal relation.

Use music cues and gain automation fitted to phrases, lyric windows and voice onsets; confirm the actual engine's behavior. Continuous music can be valid. Record gain dB, measured loudness/LUFS and true peak separately; an acceptable meter reading does not establish intelligibility or emotional fit.

## Rights and review evidence

Keep asset identity, source URL, acquisition date, applicable license/version, attribution and use scope. Separate checked provider terms, user attestation, unknown rights and actual legal review. Download price, listening membership, public synchronization, monetization, advertising and lyric display are different questions. Do not turn a source-page check into a legal guarantee. For an uncertain track use an authorized replacement or silence while rights are resolved; do not assume private-preview status authorizes acquisition or use of any recording.

Record automated checks, motion viewing, listening and user approval independently, with artifact hash and review range. Recheck changed sections and affected transitions/tails. If only frames or metadata are available, report motion/audio review pending rather than full QA passed. Keep feedback loops bounded by the project brief and demonstrated defects.
